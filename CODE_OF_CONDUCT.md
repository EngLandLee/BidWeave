# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Contributors must be respectful, specific and collaborative. Welcome differing technical perspectives; focus criticism on artifacts and evidence. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, impersonate, publish private information, or deliberately disrupt discussion, review, CI or user data.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through the security contact configured for this repository. Do not include confidential tender material. Maintainers may clarify, warn, remove content, restrict participation or end access when needed to protect contributors and users. Decisions are based on impact and available evidence and may be appealed privately.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project channels and project-related events.
